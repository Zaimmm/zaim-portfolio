01 · Ongoing

Silk Road Professionals

Business Developer
Ferghana · 2025–now

The situation

A capable Central Asian software team needed a clearer commercial story for US and other English-speaking markets. The problem was not delivery talent; it was turning broad capability into a reason for the right buyer to talk now.

My role

I moved from marketing into business development, joining positioning, website thinking, content, outbound research, offer design, and direct prospecting instead of treating them as separate departments.

What I built

  • Trigger-led ICP and account-research system for operational SMBs
  • Simpler service offers around integration, delivery rescue, and business automation
  • LinkedIn content and founder-profile direction
  • Website and case-study architecture grounded in real client work
  • Outbound sequences, qualification logic, and handoff structure

The principle

Do not ask a non-technical owner to diagnose their architecture. Start with situations they recognize, then connect the pain to the first process worth fixing.

02 · Ongoing

Candle

Brand system & website direction
Education technology · 2026–now

The situation

Candle had an umbrella brand, an established entry product, and a newer K–12 platform that needed to become the hero. The identity had to create hierarchy without making the smaller product feel abandoned.

The decision

Treat the portfolio as one family with deliberate product roles: Candle holds trust, Candle K12 carries the future-facing spotlight, and Candle Primary remains a credible entry point for smaller schools.

What I built

  • Brand architecture and product hierarchy
  • Logo exploration and identity decision system
  • Color, typography, imagery, depth, and motion directions
  • Homepage structure and product storytelling
  • Brand playbook and component-level engineering handoff

The principle

A brand family should not look equally loud everywhere. Hierarchy is not neglect; it is how people understand what matters first.

03 · Current engagement

Cultivating Impact

Growth & digital experience
Founder peer groups · 2026

The situation

The Breakthrough Groups offer was meaningful, but the digital touchpoints did not yet make the audience, value, and next step feel like one coherent journey.

The goal

Support member growth in two existing groups and create the foundation for a third while keeping the invitation broad enough for strong-fit founders beyond the existing network.

What I built

  • LinkedIn and search-performance baseline
  • Brand and website architecture
  • Homepage story and conversion path
  • Company and founder LinkedIn audits
  • Approval-led content and outreach workflow

The principle

For a trust-led offer, conversion starts before the call. Every public touchpoint should reduce ambiguity about the people, the room, and the value of joining it.
