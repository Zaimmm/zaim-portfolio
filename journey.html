Manawala · Lahore · Ferghana

The route changed. The thread did not.

The two-minute version of how learning the room, telling the truth, and building useful things became a brand and growth practice.

Roots

Manawala to Lahore

I come from Manawala near Nankana Sahib in Punjab and grew up in Lahore. Moving between a small-town origin and a large, competitive city taught me to notice context before assuming I understood it.

2021–2025

Business school, with the work already started

I completed a BBA in Marketing & Sales at Forman Christian College with a 3.64 CGPA. Community work and early client projects kept the degree grounded in people, tradeoffs, and real delivery.

2022–2025

Learning across industries and borders

Cloud School, Simon & Sons, and remote work with a US company exposed me to community development, a local trading business, and an international marketing team. I learned that the same message does not land the same way everywhere.

August 2025

Moving to Ferghana

I joined Silk Road Professionals and moved to Uzbekistan. The role grew from marketing into a broader responsibility across brand, content, website direction, and growth strategy.

2026–now

The lane becomes clear

Work with SRP, Candle, and Cultivating Impact made the pattern visible: I am strongest where brand clarity, growth thinking, design direction, and practical execution have to stay connected.
