Roots

Manawala to Lahore

I come from Manawala near Nankana Sahib in Punjab and grew up in Lahore. Moving between a small-town origin and a large, competitive city taught me to notice context before assuming I understood it.

2021–2025

Business school, with the work already started

I completed a BBA in Marketing & Sales at Forman Christian College with a 3.64 CGPA. Community work and early client projects kept the degree grounded in people, tradeoffs, and real delivery.

During university

A semester in the United States

The UGRAD exchange put me inside a different culture, classroom, and market. It made cross-cultural translation practical: the same message does not land the same way everywhere.

2022–2025

Early proof through varied work

Cloud School, Simon & Sons, and Insured Nomads exposed me to community development, a local trading business, and a US marketing team. The common lesson was simple: good ideas fail when nobody builds the operating layer.

August 2025

Moving to Ferghana

I joined Silk Road Professionals and moved to Uzbekistan. The role grew from marketing into business development across positioning, content, website direction, outbound, research, and sales.

2026–now

The lane becomes clear

Work with Candle and Cultivating Impact made the pattern visible: I am strongest where brand clarity, commercial thinking, design direction, and practical execution have to stay connected.
