[02 / Philosophy] — What I believe

What I
Believe

01 / Honest reads
Honest reads are the product

The most valuable thing I bring to a project is willingness to say "this won't work" before you've committed. That's rare, and it saves more money than good execution does.

02 / The earned lens
Comfort makes you assume. Discomfort makes you observe.

I've had more of the second one. That's not a disclaimer — it's the whole point. The sharpest reads come from people who had to actually look, because assuming wasn't an option.

03 / Compounding work
Good work compounds

A brand system built properly in 2024 still earns in 2027. A cold email sequence with a clean ICP still generates meetings six months later. The standard for "done" is whether it keeps working.
